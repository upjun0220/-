# 목재 시연 부스 — Gemini 이미지 생성용 프롬프트

> 용도: 제작도(frame_wood_sheet1/2)를 바탕으로 **예상 완성 사진(렌더)** 을 뽑기 위한 프롬프트.
> 사용법: 아래 EN 프롬프트를 Gemini(이미지 생성)에 그대로 붙여넣기. 참고 사진(성준이 렌더)이 있으면 함께 첨부하면 형태 일치도가 올라감.

---

## A. 바로 붙여넣는 프롬프트 (영문, 권장)

```
A photorealistic product-render photograph of a two-post wooden portal frame (gantry) standing on a clean bright studio/workshop concrete floor.

STRUCTURE:
- Two vertical posts made of natural pine 2x4 lumber (SPF, visible wood grain, light warm tone, matte — NOT glossy, NOT painted).
- Posts are about 2.4 m tall, spaced 1.2 m apart.
- One horizontal top beam of the same 2x4 lumber spans between the two posts near the top.
- A short diagonal brace of 2x4 lumber runs across each top corner (45 degrees), fixed with small dark metal L-angle brackets and screws.
- The bottom is completely OPEN — no bottom beam, no cross-member near the floor.
- Each post sits on a wide flat foot (2x4 laid front-to-back) with a small diagonal kick brace; a grey sandbag rests on each foot as ballast; small rubber pads under the feet.

SENSOR:
- A small dark 3D-printed plastic housing (about the size of a fist) is mounted at the center-top, screwed to the underside of the top beam, with its front face pointing straight down toward the floor. A thin black cable runs from it along the beam and down one post to a small single-board computer on the floor.

FLOOR / SCENE:
- Directly under the frame, a blue exercise/gym mat lies on the floor, framed by a red tape rectangle marking the test zone.
- Background: a tidy modern electronics workshop with shelves of equipment, soft even daylight, no harsh reflections.

STYLE:
- Realistic, clean, well-lit studio photography, eye-level straight-on front view, sharp focus, neutral white balance, professional engineering-demo look.
- Wood surfaces matte and low-reflectance. No text overlays, no watermarks, no people.
```

---

## B. 변형 옵션 (원하는 컷에 따라 한 줄 교체)

- **정면 컷** (제작도 sheet1과 동일 앵글): `eye-level straight-on front view`
- **3/4 컷** (입체감): `three-quarter view from the front-left, slight low angle`
- **디테일 컷** (상단 조인트 강조): `close-up of the top corner joint showing the diagonal brace, metal L-bracket and screws`

## C. 팁
- 성준이가 만든 참고 렌더 2장을 **레퍼런스 이미지로 첨부**하면 형태가 훨씬 잘 맞음.
- 나무가 번쩍이면 안 되므로 `matte`, `low-reflectance`, `NOT glossy` 를 꼭 유지 (알루미늄 대신 목재로 바꾼 핵심 이유 = 빛 반사 저감).
- 치수 텍스트(2.4m / 1.2m)는 이미지에 넣지 말 것 — 렌더가 지저분해짐. 치수는 제작도 sheet가 담당.
